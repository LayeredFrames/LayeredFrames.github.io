For this project I was responsible for the VFX supervision of the dream sequence, where a giant man lies in a field. To Achieve this, I used a combination of practical effects and digital compositing. The giant was created using a combination of forced perspective and digital augmentation to create the illusion of scale. The main shot was shot in 3 parts. The field background, the actress on the foreground on a bluescreen and the man on a platueau in with dirt and dried grass to make illusion of scale. The final shot was then composited together in post-production, with additional digital effects added to enhance the dreamlike quality of the scene.
