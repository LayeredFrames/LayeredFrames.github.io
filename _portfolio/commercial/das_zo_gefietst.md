For this Campaing for the Rijksoverheid (Dutch Government) I was responsible for the VFX supervision, set supervision and compositing.

The campaign consists of three different TVC's which were shot on a greenscreen so we could change the background color for each one. We added CG props that match the style and estatic of the practical props used on set. We also had to extend the greenscreen to make it an infinite world.
